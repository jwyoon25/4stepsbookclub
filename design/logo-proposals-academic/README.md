# 4steps Book Club — Academic Logo Revision

This is a separate proposal set. It does not change the committed production logo in `public/images/`, and it does not overwrite the earlier three-concept proposal.

The academic tone comes from the program's real work—close reading, evidence, teacher annotation, seminar structure, and writing—not from a borrowed university crest or prestige symbol.

## 01 — The Reader's Margin

![The Reader's Margin lockup](./01-readers-margin-lockup.svg)

**Idea:** A manuscript margin rule and a small insertion caret represent teacher feedback that helps a student write the next, stronger sentence.

- **Academic cue:** annotated reading and editorial feedback
- **Why it belongs to 4steps:** specific teacher feedback and workbooks are concrete parts of the program
- **Best quality:** most ownable connection to the actual teaching
- **Honest risk:** the insertion caret may need a one-line explanation on first exposure
- **Compact mark:** [01-readers-margin-mark.svg](./01-readers-margin-mark.svg)

## 02 — Evidence [4]

![Evidence [4] lockup](./02-evidence-lockup.svg)

**Idea:** The bracketed four reads like a literary citation marker. It connects the four-step method to thinking from evidence in the text.

- **Academic cue:** citation and textual evidence
- **Why it belongs to 4steps:** the brand teaches students to support thinking with what they read
- **Best quality:** clearest academic signal and strongest small-size mark
- **Honest risk:** it can initially read as “reference four” or code syntax; the serif drawing prevents most of that ambiguity
- **Compact mark:** [02-evidence-mark.svg](./02-evidence-mark.svg)

## 03 — The Syllabus

![The Syllabus lockup](./03-syllabus-lockup.svg)

**Idea:** `4. steps` is typeset like a numbered seminar section. The horizontal rule and disciplined spacing present the method as a considered curriculum.

- **Academic cue:** syllabus and journal-section architecture
- **Why it belongs to 4steps:** the four stages are a structured course, not four decorative brand motifs
- **Best quality:** most restrained, disciplined, and least like a generic app logo
- **Honest risk:** it is cooler and less immediately warm than The Reader's Margin
- **Compact mark:** [03-syllabus-mark.svg](./03-syllabus-mark.svg)

## Starting recommendation

Start with **The Reader's Margin** if the goal is an academic identity that still feels human and specific to 4steps. Choose **Evidence [4]** if immediate scholarly credibility and a memorable favicon matter most. Choose **The Syllabus** if the brand should feel calm, rigorous, and almost publication-like.

The master color is Library Green (`#17342F`). The brick accent (`#8A3B32`) is deliberately limited to a proofreader's caret or course rule. Every essential word remains green. The individual SVGs use outlined vector lettering, so they do not depend on an installed font.

Avoid crests, shields, seals, laurels, Latin mottos, founding dates, and navy-and-gold collegiate styling. Those cues can imply institutional affiliation the business does not claim.
